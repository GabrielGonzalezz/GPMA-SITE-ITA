Ariangelo Hauer Dias Filho is a Ph.D. candidate in Aeronautical and Mechanical Engineering at ITA (since 2024), supported by CNPq. His doctoral research, *Numerical modelling of the L-PBF process and optimal design for additive manufacturing*, simulates laser powder bed fusion and uses asymptotic homogenisation and response surfaces to design support structures that reduce distortion. It is linked to the metal additive manufacturing line (ADAM) of the CPE-FLYMOV research centre.

He received his M.Sc. from ITA in 2023 with the dissertation *3D printed bimaterial honeycomb structures: design proposition, asymptotic homogenization and properties testing*, co-supervised by Dr Andrew Gleadall (Loughborough University) with a CAPES scholarship, and holds a degree in Materials Engineering from the Universidade Estadual de Ponta Grossa (2017).
