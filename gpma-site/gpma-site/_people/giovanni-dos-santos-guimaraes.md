Giovanni dos Santos Guimarães is an M.Sc. student in Aeronautical and Mechanical Engineering at ITA. His research investigates surface residual stresses in titanium parts produced by laser powder bed fusion (L-PBF), aiming at the optimisation of additively manufactured components for future aircraft within the metal additive manufacturing line of the CPE-FLYMOV research centre.
