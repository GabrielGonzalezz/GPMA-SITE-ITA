Thiago Assis Dutra was one of the group's first doctoral students. He received his Ph.D. from ITA in 2020 with a thesis on the mechanical behaviour of 3D-printed continuous carbon-fibre reinforced thermoplastics, covering experimental characterisation, asymptotic homogenisation, representative volume elements and failure criteria, partly developed with Chalmers University of Technology.

He is now an Assistant Professor in the Department of Electromechanical Engineering at the Universidade da Beira Interior, Portugal, and continues to collaborate with GPMA on the design of printed fibre composites.
