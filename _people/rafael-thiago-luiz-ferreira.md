Rafael Thiago Luiz Ferreira is an Associate Professor in the Department of Design of the Mechanical Engineering Division at the Instituto Tecnológico de Aeronáutica (ITA), where he has coordinated GPMA since 2016.

He holds a degree in Mechanical Technology (Design) from the Faculdade de Tecnologia de São Paulo (2003), and an M.Sc. (2008) and Ph.D. (2013) in Aeronautical and Mechanical Engineering from ITA, including a research stay at Instituto Superior Técnico, Lisbon (2012). In 2018–2019 he was a FAPESP-funded postdoctoral visiting researcher at the Centre for Additive Manufacturing of the University of Nottingham, UK.

His research covers additive manufacturing, structural optimisation and composite materials, with a focus on high-performance materials and structures: fibre-reinforced thermoplastics, the titanium alloy Ti-6Al-4V and optimised periodic lattice structures. He takes part in the ADAM line (Advanced Design for Metal Additive Manufacturing) of the CPE-FLYMOV research centre. He teaches Theory of Structures (EST-24, EST-31) and the graduate courses Optimisation in Mechanical Engineering (MP-288) and Optimal Design in Additive Manufacturing (MP-289), and received the Weiss Prize for teaching excellence four times (2015–2018).
