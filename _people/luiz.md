Luis Rigon is an undergraduate student in Mechanical-Aeronautical Engineering at the Instituto Tecnológico de Aeronáutica (ITA) and an undergraduate researcher at GPMA. He won bronze medals at the Brazilian Public Schools Mathematics Olympiad (OBMEP) in 2014, 2015 and 2018, with an honourable mention in 2013, and took part in the Junior Scientific Initiation Program (PIC). His research interests include mechanical engineering, additive manufacturing, structures and computational simulation.
