Rafael Quelho de Macedo completed both his M.Sc. (2016) and Ph.D. (2020) at ITA under Prof. Rafael Thiago Luiz Ferreira, with FAPESP scholarships. His M.Sc. developed a failure criterion for unidirectional fibre composites based on asymptotic homogenisation; his Ph.D. produced VOLCO and VOLCO-X, voxel-based simulations of material distribution and voids in extrusion additive manufacturing, in collaboration with Loughborough University and the University of Nottingham.
